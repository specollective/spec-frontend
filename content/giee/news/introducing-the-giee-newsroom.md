The GIEE newsroom is a space for sharing the work behind a more inclusive and accountable approach to emerging technology.

## What you will find here

We will publish updates from our ecosystem, notes from active research, and reflections from the educators, researchers, and community partners shaping this work.

The goal is simple: make the questions, evidence, and decisions that guide GIEE easier to follow and easier to join.

> Inclusive governance is built through shared understanding and public learning.

This article is written in Markdown and lives in `content/giee/news`. New articles can be added there with a title, date, excerpt, and optional author in the frontmatter.
